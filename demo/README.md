Replay of recorded KnowWhen-R test runs. Pick a model and a question from the test split and see what the closed book answer, the retrieved answer and the probes said, and what the system decided at a chosen share of questions answered. Nothing is generated live: every answer and score comes from the saved runs.

Code and report: https://github.com/Optimus2007/knowwhen-r. Probes: `kungfupanda12/knowwhen-r-probes`. Traces: `kungfupanda12/knowwhen-r-traces`.
