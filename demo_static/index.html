KnowWhen-R

Probes on a frozen model's activations decide whether to answer from memory, use retrieved Wikipedia passages, or abstain. This page replays recorded runs on the 3,968 test questions. Nothing is generated here, and the retrieved answer is shown for every question so you can compare. Cuts come from validation rows, so the share answered is close to the target, not exact.
